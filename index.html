Prepare your spreadsheet in three easy steps

  1. Organize your columns. Add headers such as Name, Company, Address Line 1, Address Line 2, City, State, ZIP, and Country. Only the columns you fill will be used on the labels.
  2. Keep one household per row. Each row becomes one label. If you have more than 30 rows, the generator automatically creates additional sheets.
  3. Export to Excel format. Save your file as .xlsx or .xls (most spreadsheet apps have an Export or Download option). Then upload the file above.
