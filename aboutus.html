Avery 5160 Label Maker Generator Workflow & Tips

Workflow & Best Practices

Whether you are preparing holiday mailers or shipping kits for an event, this companion guide walks you through the end-to-end process of transforming spreadsheet data into ready-to-print Avery 5160 labels using the generator.

Launch the label generator

Plan your data pipeline

  1. Collect addresses in a shared spreadsheet. Choose a template that already includes headers for Name, Company, Address Line 1, Address Line 2, City, State, ZIP, and Country so collaborators can easily fill them in.
  2. Validate before export. Sort and filter the sheet to catch missing ZIP codes or duplicated recipients. It’s much easier to fix inconsistencies before converting to labels.
  3. Save as Excel. Download the final version as .xlsx (or .xls). The generator reads the first worksheet automatically.

Printing checklist

  • Load Avery 5160 label sheets with the printable side up (check your printer’s feed direction).
  • After uploading in the generator, review the preview for long lines that might truncate and shorten text if needed.
  • Use your browser’s print dialog and set the scale to 100% (or “Actual Size”) so the layout stays aligned with the labels.
  • Disable headers/footers in the print dialog for a clean finish.
  • Print a single test sheet on plain paper before using label stock and hold it against a blank label page to confirm alignment.

Tips for larger mail merges

Batch your exports

If you manage thousands of rows, split the spreadsheet into 200–300 row chunks. This keeps the browser responsive and generates manageable sets of printable sheets.

Use filters to target segments

Create filtered copies of your workbook (for example, by region or campaign) and upload only the rows that are relevant to the current label run.

Archive your source file

Store the exported workbook next to your mailing records so you can revisit or reprint labels later without rebuilding the sheet.

Troubleshooting

If the preview appears blank, confirm that your headers are in the first row and that each column contains data. The generator only displays lines with text to keep labels tidy.

For international mailings, the country line only appears when the country is something other than the United States. This matches USPS recommendations and leaves more room for addresses.

Need more help? Draft a quick test sheet with a handful of rows and experiment in the generator before committing to the full mailing list.
